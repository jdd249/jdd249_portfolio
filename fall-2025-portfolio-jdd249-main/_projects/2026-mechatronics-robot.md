## Overview
This project involved designing, building, and programming an autonomous mechatronics robot to collect cubes in a competitive arena. Our team prioritized simplicity, reliability, and rapid cube collection, using a custom 3D-printed chassis and wheels, DC motors, QTI edge sensors, and a front-mounted color sensor. The robot followed a sensor-based sweeping strategy to navigate the board, detect color changes, avoid boundaries, and collect cubes efficiently, ultimately placing first in its round-robin bracket.
